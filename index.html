Meet XSTUDY®.

XSTUDY® is a student-focused education technology startup founded by Nwahiri-Godwin Tochukwu. Its vision is to create a smarter, more engaging digital learning experience that helps students access learning resources, stay organised and make meaningful academic progress. The platform is being developed with an ambition to serve students in Nigeria and, over time, beyond.
